# Stage 26b: TabPFN on the color numbers plus DINOv2 or VisionFM features

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.tabpfn_image` at commit `1cf698e`; settings in `configs/tabpfn_image.yaml`. The mentors asked about DINOv2; TabPFN on 29 color numbers is our best eyelid model, so this tests whether image-model features add to it.

- **Anemia:** Ghana children (CP-AnemiC, the 388 photos with no copy): Hb < 11.0 g/dL. India and Italy (Eyes-Defy adults): WHO by sex, women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL. One cutoff per dataset; nothing is carried between datasets.
- **Image features:** frozen DINOv2-small (CLS + mean patch, 768 numbers) and VisionFM (eye-pretrained), from the caches; scaled and squeezed to 16 PCA numbers inside each training fold.
- **Split:** 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by photo (one photo per person); Ghana also leaves one hospital out. Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples; changes are paired (same photos). **train** = AUROC on the fold's own training photos.
- **Answer, fixed before running:** image features help if color + image beats color alone (paired CI above 0).

## Answer

Color + image beats color alone for: none.
