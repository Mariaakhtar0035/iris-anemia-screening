- **Cross-validation artifact within regions (found after the first run; the analysis is unchanged, only how it is read).** A village's out-of-fold score is learned without that village. When a high-anemia village is in the test fold, its region's training average is a little lower, so its own score is a little lower. Inside one region the region baseline's scores differ only by this (0.221-0.239 in one region of 36 villages), which gives it a strongly negative within-region correlation. The baseline's true value is 0 (it cannot rank villages inside a region), so the within-region column is read against 0. Models that use region (top 10, all questions, Hb ranking, TabPFN top 10 and all) carry a smaller share of the same downward pull within regions; base 6 has no region question and is least affected. The locked test has no such artifact (all its villages are left out of training equally), so its within-region numbers are the cleaner ones, though descriptive.
- **The winner's numbers are optimistic:** it was picked as Step 5's winner using these same building villages. Base 6 and the region baselines have no such selection.
