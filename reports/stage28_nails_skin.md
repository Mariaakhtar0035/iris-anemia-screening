# Stage 28: fingernail and skin color numbers together

Generated by `python -m src.nails_skin` at commit `4d9a566`; settings in `configs/nails_skin.yaml`. Data: Yakimov et al. 2024 (Moscow adults, one photo each in a fixed light box).

- **Anemic = Hb < 12.0 g/dL (120 g/L) for everyone**, 69 of 250 (no sex column, so one adult cutoff; men between 12.0 and 13.0 count as not anemic).
- **Inputs:** the 29 color numbers of the nail boxes, of the skin boxes just behind each nail, both side by side in one model (58 numbers), and the mean of the nail model's and the skin model's scores. For the untrained rule, the mean of the two redness ranks.
- **Baselines:** dummy, the redness rule, and a random forest on the box sizes only (no color).
- **Splits:** 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by patient, and leave one photo session out. **train** = AUROC on the fold's own training photos. Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples; **change vs nail** is paired (same people).
- **Answer, fixed before running:** together they help if nail + skin beats the nail alone with the CI of the change above 0.

## Answer
