# Stage 31: how much of the Ghana score is real anemia signal once the folder trace is taken out?

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.trace_free` at commit `82d7e29`; settings and the answers (fixed before running) in `configs/trace_free.yaml`.

- **Cutoffs, one per dataset:** Ghana eyelids, nails, palms: children 6-59 months, **Hb < 11.0 g/dL**. India and Italy eyelids (Eyes-Defy adults): **WHO by sex, women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL**, pregnancy not recorded. Moscow nails (adults, no sex column): **Hb < 12.0 g/dL** for everyone.
- **Input:** the same 29 color numbers. 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by photo / patient / child, scores averaged over repeats; Ghana eyelids also leave one hospital out. Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples. Hb models estimate Hb minus the person's own cutoff; the score is minus the estimate.
- **Within-folder ridge:** in each training fold, each label group's mean is taken out of the 29 numbers and of Hb, and ridge is fit on what is left, so only differences inside a group shape the weights. Test photos are scored as they are (their label is never used). A trace shared by a whole folder can't be learned this way.
