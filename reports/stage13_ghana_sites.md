# Stage 13: palm and fingernail photos of Ghanaian children

Generated by `python -m src.ghana_sites` at commit `bca52df`; settings in `configs/ghana_sites.yaml`. Mentor advice M9, M12 (`docs/eyelid_followup.md`). Data: Asare, Appiahene, Donkoh 2022, Mendeley Data 10.17632/ccr8cm22vz.1 (palm) and 10.17632/2xx4j3kjg2.1 (fingernails), CC BY 4.0.

- **Anemic = the dataset's own label**, read from the file name. The archives have no Hb, age, sex or hospital, so the cutoff behind the label isn't stated (the group's eyelid set uses Hb < 11.0 g/dL for children). Nothing links a palm or nail photo to an eyelid photo, so the body sites can't be combined per child.
- **One row per photo.** Each photo is stored 6 or 12 times (exact copies plus 90/180/270-degree rotations); those are merged by a rotation-aware hash, then near-copies by the CP-AnemiC audit's rule (16x16 difference hash within 20 bits, size within 5%). Photos filed under both labels are dropped. Rotations don't change color numbers, so the copies add nothing as augmentation.

| site | files | exact_photos | photos | near_copy_links | photos_under_several_names | dropped_conflicting_labels | kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| palm | 4260 | 516 | 515 | 1 | 11 | 1 | 514 |
| fingernails | 4260 | 532 | 526 | 6 | 21 | 0 | 526 |

- **Input:** the 29 color numbers of the eyelid stages. Palm: every pixel of the patch (no transparency). Nails: the opaque pixels of the cut-out.
- `rule_low_redness`: no training, score = minus mean a* (paler = more likely anemic).
- `negative_control_rf_no_color`: random forest on image size and cut-out shape only.
- `naming_style_only`: logistic regression on the file-naming style alone (below). Well above 0.5 means the styles differ in who was photographed, a batch shortcut.
- **AUROC inside the main style** re-scores the same held-out scores on the largest naming style only, where the style can't help.
- **Split:** repeated stratified 5-fold CV by photo (5 repeats, scores averaged). **train** = AUROC of each fold's model on its own training photos, mean (min-max). Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples. Share tested to catch 90%: testing at random needs 90%; lower is better.
