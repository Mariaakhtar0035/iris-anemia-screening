# TabPFN shortcut checks

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.tabpfn_checks` at commit `98ad699`. Follow-up to M14 (`docs/eyelid_followup.md`, Step 12): is TabPFN's high score pallor color, or something it can read without color?

- **Labels:** CP-AnemiC Hb < 11.0 g/dL, the 388 photos with no copy (as in Stage 9). Ghana palm and nails: the dataset's own label from the file name, cutoff not stated (Stage 13).
- **no color (size and shape):** image width, height, aspect and the opaque and edge shares of the cut-out, the inputs of the random-forest no-color control. Near 0.5 = no shortcut TabPFN can find there.
- **leave one hospital out:** CP-AnemiC only (10 hospitals); pooled held-out scores, so a hospital's photos are never in training when it is scored. Close to the 5x5 CV score = not leaning on the hospital.
- **hospital only:** logistic regression on the hospital alone, within-hospital 5x5 CV: what knowing the hospital is worth by itself.
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples. **train** = AUROC of each fold's model on its own training photos, mean (min-max).
