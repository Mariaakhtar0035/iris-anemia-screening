Generated by `python -m src.survey.select_features` at commit `2ff02bc`; settings in `configs/feature_selection.yaml`. Zimbabwe only, no transfer.

- **Women:** 9265 aged 15-49 with an Hb result, 27.3% anemic.
- **Anemic = DHS `v457` at any severity:** WHO cutoffs for women on Hb adjusted for altitude and smoking, < 12.0 g/dL, or < 11.0 g/dL when pregnant.
- **Extra target, the Hb number:** `v456` (adjusted Hb, mean 12.7 g/dL, range 2.9-19.0). With the same cutoffs it reproduces `v457` for all 9265 women. It is a target only, never an input; a predicted Hb gets the woman's own cutoff afterwards.
