# Stage 12: fingernail photos under fixed lighting

Generated by `python -m src.nails` at commit `c2e8f21`; settings in `configs/nails.yaml`. Mentor advice M9, M12 (`docs/eyelid_followup.md`). Data: Yakimov et al. 2024, *Scientific Data* (figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.c.6760179): adults in Moscow, one photo each, taken in a fixed LED box.

- **Anemic = Hb < 12.0 g/dL (120 g/L) for everyone**, 69 of 250. The per-patient file has no sex column, so one adult cutoff is used (the WHO cutoff for women, chosen by Jaineel): men between 12.0 and 13.0 count as not anemic.
- **Input:** the pixels inside the three nail boxes of each photo, pooled; the same 29 color numbers as the eyelid stages.
- `rule_low_redness`: no training, score = minus mean a* (paler nail = more likely anemic).
- `negative_control_rf_no_color`: random forest on the nail box sizes only. Above 0.5 means either a leak or that hand size (and so sex, which shifts Hb) carries the label.
- `session_only`: logistic regression on the photo session alone (hashed `MEASUREMENT_DATE`, 20 sessions of 1-26 people, anemic share 0%-100%, 1 with no anemic person). Well above 0.5 means sessions differ in who was photographed, a shortcut a color model could use.
- **Splits:** repeated stratified 5-fold CV by patient (5 repeats, scores averaged), and leave one session out (pooled out-of-fold scores; the dummy can fall below 0.5 there because each fold's prior comes from the other sessions). **train** = AUROC of each fold's model on its own training photos, mean (min-max).
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples. Share tested to catch 90%: testing at random needs 90%; lower is better.
