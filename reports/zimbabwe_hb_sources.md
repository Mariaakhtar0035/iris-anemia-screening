# Stage 29 Step 8d: where the Hb ranking's gain comes from, and the clinic number

Generated by `python -m src.survey.hb_sources` at commit `2ff02bc`; settings in `configs/feature_selection.yaml` (`hb_sources`). Building villages, the same 5 x 5 repeated village folds as Step 8a; village-bootstrap 95% CIs and paired differences, 2000 resamples.

- **Primary target: any anemia**, DHS `v457` 1-3: Hb adjusted for altitude and smoking < 12.0 g/dL, or < 11.0 g/dL if pregnant (WHO).
- **Second target: moderate or severe**, DHS `v457` 1-2: adjusted Hb **< 10.0 g/dL for every woman, pregnant or not** (DHS: moderate 7.0-9.9, severe < 7.0). For pregnant women this matches WHO's moderate cutoff; for non-pregnant women it is stricter than WHO's < 11.0. Pre-registered in Step 9.
- **Hb ranking:** a gradient boosting regressor predicts adjusted Hb; women are ranked by her cutoff minus the prediction (12.0/11.0 for any anemia, 10.0 for moderate or severe). Same model settings for every question set: base 6 (6 questions), base 6 + region `v024` (7), all (482).
- **Clinic number:** testing women from the highest score down, the share of all women who must be tested to catch 90% of cases, and the share of those tested who have the outcome. Random testing needs 90%.
