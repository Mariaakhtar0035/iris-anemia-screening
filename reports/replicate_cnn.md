# Replication: the CP-AnemiC paper's joint ResNet50

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.replicate_cnn` at commit `1fd7b65` on `mps`. Settings and the deviations from the paper are in `configs/replicate.yaml`. Anemia = Hb < 11.0 g/dL. All 710 rows, as in the paper. Bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples) resample whole photo groups.

- `paper_random_rows`: stratified 5-fold CV over rows, the paper's protocol. Copies of a photo can sit in both train and test.
- `grouped_by_photo`: identical, except every copy of a photo stays in one fold.
