Generated by `python -m src.survey.severity` at commit `2ff02bc`; settings in `configs/feature_selection.yaml` (`severity_target`), fixed on 2026-11-16 before any model was run on it.

- **Target: DHS `v457` codes 1-2**, moderate or severe: Hb adjusted for altitude and smoking **< 10.0 g/dL for every woman, pregnant or not** (DHS convention: moderate 7.0-9.9, severe < 7.0; checked to reproduce `v457` for all women). Not the WHO 2011 cutoffs. The main analysis (any anemia, < 12.0 g/dL or < 11.0 if pregnant) is unchanged; this is a second analysis next to it.
- **653 women (7.0%)**: 528 in the building villages, 125 in the locked test. At this rate a PR-AUC of 0.07 is chance (the dummy), and testing a random 30% finds 30% of cases.
- Models fixed in advance, no new feature selection. The Hb ranking scores women by how far their predicted Hb falls below 10.0 g/dL (Step 8a's Hb regressor). Rankings have no probability, so no Brier score.
- 5 x 5 repeated village CV on the building villages (Step 8a's folds); each woman's score is the average over repeats. Village-bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples.
