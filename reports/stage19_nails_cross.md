# Stage 19: fingernail models across sites (Ghana children <-> Moscow adults)

Generated by `python -m src.nails_cross` at commit `137d241`; settings in `configs/nails_cross.yaml`. Mentor advice (2026-10-31): try both cross-country and per-region.

- **Ghana fingernails** (Asare et al. 2022): 526 distinct photos of children under 6 after merging copies and rotations (as in Stage 13). **Anemic = the dataset's own label from the file name**; there is no Hb, so the cutoff behind it is not stated.
- **Moscow fingernails** (Yakimov et al. 2024): 250 adults in one fixed light box. **Anemic = Hb < 12.0 g/dL for everyone** (no sex column). Color numbers of the three nail boxes as photographed.
- **The two definitions of anemia differ** (children vs adults, a file-name label vs a lab cutoff), so a model trained on one is judged against the other's definition.
- **Within** rows: each dataset alone, 5x5 CV by photo. **Across** rows: fit on all of one dataset, applied unchanged to the other. `standardized per dataset` scales each dataset's 29 color numbers to mean 0, sd 1 on its own photos (no labels). The rule is minus mean a*, no training.
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples, one photo per person.
