# Stage 8: transfer from Zimbabwe to Malawi and Zambia

Generated by `python -m src.survey.transfer`. Models fit on all 9265 tested women from Zimbabwe 2015 (preprocessing fit on Zimbabwe only) and applied unchanged to Malawi 2024 and Zambia 2024. Anemic = DHS `v457` at any severity (WHO cutoffs on altitude- and smoking-adjusted Hb) in every country. Bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples) resample whole clusters. Calibration slope 1 and intercept 0 are ideal.

The DHS wealth index is built separately for each country and survey, so the "richest" quintile in Zambia is not the same wealth as in Zimbabwe, and the Zimbabwe survey is 9 years older than the other two.
