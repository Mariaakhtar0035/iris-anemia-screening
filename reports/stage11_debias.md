# Stage 11: taking the India/Italy shortcut out

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.debias`; settings in `configs/debias.yaml`. Mentor advice M5-M8 (`docs/eyelid_followup.md`). Eyes-Defy: India 95 and Italy 122 photos, anemic = WHO adult cutoffs by sex (women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL); 71.6% of India and 18.9% of Italy anemic. Color features from the hand-segmented palpebral conjunctiva. 5-fold CV x 5 repeats stratified by country x anemia, scores averaged over repeats; bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples).

- **Italy early batch**: folders 1-31 only (31 photos, 21 anemic). Almost all Italian anemia is in this batch (Stage 10), so this is the Italy check that recognizing the batch can't pass.
- **Rank gap**: mean percentile rank of Indian minus Italian photos, among people with the same anemia status. 0 = once anemia is fixed, the model doesn't care about the country.
- **TPR / FPR gap**: India minus Italy, at the one threshold that catches 90% of anemic people overall.
