# Stage 15: how many local photos does a new country need?

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.few_local` at commit `974267c`; settings in `configs/few_local.yaml`. A model trained on two countries doesn't beat the redness rule on the third (Stage 14). If a clinic in the new country blood-tests k people and labels their photos, how large must k be?

- **Photos:** the 605 of Stage 14, one per person. Labels mixed by design: Ghana children Hb < 11.0 g/dL; India and Italy adults WHO by sex (women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL; pregnancy not recorded).
- **Input:** the 29 color numbers of the hand-segmented palpebral conjunctiva only.
- **Split:** for each target country, 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by photo, stratified by anemia; k = 0, 10, 20, 40, all labelled photos drawn at random (stratified) from the training part; scores averaged over repeats. Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples.
- **other two + k local:** the other two countries plus the k photos, each country standardized with its own mean and SD (the new country's from its unlabelled training photos, never the test photos). k = 0 is Stage 14's train-on-two, with stricter standardization.
- **k local only:** the k photos alone, no other country.
- **AUROC change:** paired bootstrap (same resampled photos) against the rule, and between the two ways of training for the same model.
- **Italy early batch:** folders 1-31; local Italy photos can teach a model the batch, so this is the Italy test the batch can't pass.
- **Answer, fixed before running:** the smallest k at which a model beats the rule (CI of the change above 0), and whether the other two countries help at the same k.

**Smallest k that beats the rule:** Ghana: all; India: no k; Italy: no k; Italy early batch: no k.
