## Step 3: how strong is each question on its own?

- **Score:** a woman's group's anemia rate (her answer; numbers cut into 10 equal-count groups, blank and special codes as their own groups), learned on the other villages (5-fold by village), pulled toward the overall rate as if each group had 20 more average women. 0.5 = no better than chance.
- **Hb error cut:** the same groups' mean Hb as an estimate; how much it lowers the average error (g/dL) compared with guessing everyone's Hb as the overall mean. Positive = helps.
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples of whole villages.

## Step 4: questions that repeat each other

A question is dropped when a question already kept nearly fixes its answer: among women who answered both (at least 200), knowing the kept answer removes at least 95% of the mistakes in guessing it (Goodman-Kruskal lambda). It is one-way, so a dropped question adds almost nothing the kept one doesn't already say. Questions are visited from fewest blanks to most, then strongest in Step 3 first.
