# Stage 27: does the eyelid model catch the sickest children?

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.severity` at commit `ca2732a`; settings in `configs/severity.yaml`.

- **Children:** Ghana, CP-AnemiC, the 388 photos with no copy. **Anemic: Hb < 11.0 g/dL** (WHO, 6-59 months).
- **Bands:** WHO severity grades for this age: severe < 7.0, moderate 7.0-9.9, mild 10.0-10.9 g/dL; healthy children split at 12.0.
- **Scores:** held-out, 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by photo (Stage 9's folds), averaged over repeats. Models on the 29 color numbers, plus the untrained redness rule.
- **Flagged** = sent for a blood test. Two thresholds, each set on all 388 children: one clears 90% of healthy children, one catches 90% of anemic children. For anemic bands, flagged = caught; for healthy bands, flagged = false alarm. **Baseline:** a score with no signal flags 10% of every band at the first threshold and 90% at the second.
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples; the threshold is set again in every resample.
- **Answer, fixed before running:** the misses are mostly mild cases if, for the best model, caught among moderate + severe minus caught among mild has its CI above 0 (first threshold).

## Answer

Are the misses mostly mild cases (tabpfn)? **Not shown**: moderate + severe minus mild = -0.043 [-0.172, 0.129].

At the first threshold tabpfn misses 40 of 165 anemic children: 3 severe, 25 moderate, 12 mild. Median Hb of the missed children 8.9 g/dL, of the caught 9.5 g/dL.
