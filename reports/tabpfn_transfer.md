# TabPFN across datasets

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.tabpfn_transfer` at commit `a9390db`. Does the best Ghana eyelid model (TabPFN, 0.919 within CP-AnemiC, `stage9_ceiling.md`) still work on another dataset, unchanged? Same setup as Stage 5 (`stage5_cross_dataset.md`).

- **Labels:** CP-AnemiC, the 388 photos with no copy, anemic = Hb < 11.0 g/dL (children). Eyes-Defy, 217 photos with Hb, anemic = WHO adult cutoffs by sex (women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL; pregnancy not recorded). The cutoffs differ between the datasets by design.
- **Input:** the 29 color numbers of the hand-segmented palpebral conjunctiva on both sides. Each model is fit on all of one dataset and applied unchanged to the other. `rule_low_redness`: minus mean a*, no training.
- **Italy early batch:** folders 1-31 (31 photos, 21 anemic). Almost all Italian anemia is in this batch (Stage 10), so it is the Italy test that recognizing the batch can't pass.
- Bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples, one photo per person. Share tested to catch 90%: testing in random order needs 90%; lower is better.
