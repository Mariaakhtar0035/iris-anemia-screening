Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.hb_local` at commit `ba4799f + uncommitted changes`; settings in `configs/hb_local.yaml`.

- **Model:** regressors trained once on all 388 CP-AnemiC photos (Ghana children, 29 color numbers), applied to Eyes-Defy (Stage 17). **Anemic at the new site = WHO by sex: women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL**; each person is judged against their own cutoff.
- **Each draw:** k people at the site (India or Italy) are picked at random and have a lab Hb. `shift` adds their mean error to every estimate; `shift and scale` fits a straight line from estimate to lab Hb on them. Everything is scored on the **rest** of the site, never on the k people used to fit. 200 draws per k; numbers are the mean with the 2.5-97.5% range over draws (not a bootstrap).
- **Comparisons:** no correction; a regressor trained on the k local people alone; the untrained redness rule.
- A shift keeps everyone's order, so it cannot change the AUROC; only `shift and scale` can (it reverses the order when the fitted slope is negative).
- **Italy can't be trusted on its own:** 21 of its 23 anemic patients are in one early photo batch (Stage 10).
