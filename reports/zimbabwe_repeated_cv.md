# Stage 29 Step 8a: repeated cross-validation with region baselines

Generated by `python -m src.survey.repeated_cv` at commit `2ff02bc`; settings in `configs/feature_selection.yaml` (`review`). **Building villages only:** 7416 women in 321 villages, 5-fold by village repeated 5 times. Anemic = DHS `v457` (WHO for women on Hb adjusted for altitude and smoking: < 12.0 g/dL, < 11.0 if pregnant).

- Each woman's score is the average of her out-of-fold scores over the repeats. CIs and paired differences: village bootstrap of those averaged scores, 2000 resamples (not the fold-to-fold spread, because repeated-CV folds overlap).
- **Region baselines use no model:** the anemia share among training women in her region (10 regions), or her region x urban/rural cell, pulled toward the overall share as if each group had 20 more average women.
- **Hb ranking:** a gradient boosting regressor predicts her adjusted Hb from all questions; her score is her own cutoff (12.0 g/dL, 11.0 if pregnant) minus that prediction, so women predicted furthest below their cutoff rank first. It is a ranking, not a probability, so no Brier score.
- **The winner's numbers are optimistic:** top 10 by importance was picked as Step 5's winner using these same building villages. Its questions are chosen inside each training fold, but the decision to use this method was not. Base 6 and the region baselines have no such selection.
