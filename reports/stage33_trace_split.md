# Stage 33: how much of each score is real? The score split into a smooth Hb part and a step at the cutoff

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.trace_split` at commit `0c0c782`; settings and the answers (fixed before running) in `configs/trace_split.yaml`.

- **Cutoffs, one per dataset:** Ghana eyelids, nails, palms: children 6-59 months, **Hb < 11.0 g/dL**. India and Italy eyelids (Eyes-Defy adults): **WHO by sex, women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL**, pregnancy not recorded. Moscow nails (no sex column): **Hb < 12.0 g/dL for everyone**.
- **Idea:** pallor changes smoothly with Hb, so a pallor reader's average score has no step exactly at the cutoff. The step (anemic side minus healthy side at the cutoff, from a straight line on each side within 2.0 g/dL) is the folder trace. Taking it out of every anemic person's score leaves the part that follows Hb. Scores are ranks mapped to a standard normal, so the step is in score SDs.
- 5-fold CV x 5 repeats by photo / patient / child; bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples (people resampled, the step refit each time). Real-signal share = (trace-free AUROC - 0.5) / (AUROC - 0.5).
