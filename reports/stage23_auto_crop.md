# Stage 23: a vision model that crops the eyelid and flags photos to retake

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.auto_crop` at commit `2ff204e`; settings in `configs/auto_crop.yaml`. Mentor advice (2026-10-31): put vision models in the data pipeline, one that crops the region being analyzed and one that separates good from bad photos. Per location only.

- **Photos:** Eyes-Defy, the only set with the full photo next to a hand-drawn palpebral mask: India 94, Italy 121 (Italy_15 and India_2 left out: broken mask). Adults, **WHO by sex: women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL** (pregnancy not recorded).
- **Vision model:** frozen DINOv2-small; photo resized to a long side of 448 px, one token per 14 px patch. A logistic regression learns from the hand masks which patches are eyelid, 5-fold CV by photo inside each location, so no photo is cropped by a model that saw its mask. Probabilities cut at 0.5, largest piece kept. No anemia label is used. **Confidence** = mean probability inside its crop.
- **Anemia:** the same 29 color numbers and models as before, from the hand mask or the automatic crop on the same photo pixels; 5x5 CV by photo; bootstrap 95% CIs, 2000 resamples; changes are paired.
- **Retake gate:** the least confident 10% or 20% of photos are dropped at test time (no labels, no mask).
