# Stage 30: cleaning the Malawi survey with the Stage 29 rules

Generated by `python -m src.survey.select_features` at commit `938254d + uncommitted changes`; settings in `configs/feature_selection.yaml`. Malawi only, no transfer.

- **Women:** 10000 aged 15-49 with an Hb result, 26.1% anemic.
- **Anemic = DHS `v457` at any severity:** WHO cutoffs for women on Hb adjusted for altitude and smoking, < 12.0 g/dL, or < 11.0 g/dL when pregnant.
- **Extra target, the Hb number:** `v456` (adjusted Hb, mean 12.6 g/dL, range 2.8-18.5). With the same cutoffs it reproduces `v457` for all 10000 women. It is a target only, never an input; a predicted Hb gets the woman's own cutoff afterwards.
- Steps 1-2 never look at how a column relates to anemia, so nothing here can leak the answer.
