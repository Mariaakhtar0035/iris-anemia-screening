# Stage 5: eyelid models across datasets

Generated by `python -m src.eyelid.cross_dataset`. Settings in `configs/cross_dataset.yaml`. Input on both sides is the hand-segmented palpebral conjunctiva (CP-AnemiC cut-outs; Eyes-Defy `*_palpebral.png` masks cropped to the conjunctiva). CP-AnemiC: the 388 photos with no copy, anemic = Hb < 11.0 g/dL (children). Eyes-Defy: 217 photos with Hb, anemic = WHO adult cutoffs by sex (women < 12.0, men < 13.0 g/dL; pregnancy not recorded), which is **not** the ViT paper's labelling used in the replication. The cutoffs differ between the datasets by design. Across datasets, models are fit on all of one dataset and applied unchanged. Bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples, one photo per patient). `rule_low_redness`: minus mean a*, no training. `rule_country`: India = anemic.
