## What each variant is

- **A.** Each woman's rank under the Hb ranking and under TabPFN classification (all questions), averaged 50/50.
- **B.** The Hb regressor's mean plus a second regressor predicting the size of its error (fitted on out-of-fold errors inside each training fold); P(anemic) = P(Hb below her own cutoff) under a normal error. The constant-spread version uses one error size for everyone.
- **C.** Base 6 + region (`v024`): logistic regression, and an ordinal logistic regression on the four DHS levels (proportional odds) scored as P(moderate or severe).
- **D.** The all-questions Hb regressor plus three village averages of answers: mean wealth score (`v191`), share with secondary or higher education (`v106`), share using a modern method (`v313`).
- **E.** TabPFN 3.5 predicting the Hb number from all questions, ranked by her cutoff minus the prediction. Run because Step 8e showed the Hb target beats yes/no for gradient boosting (the condition stated before Step 8e's results).
